Pizza Glow

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 20 October 2026

Developer: Cordunianu Florin

This policy covers Pizza Glow, the Android pizza restaurant game currently titled Pizzeria inside the app, package com.florin.pizzaglow. Cordunianu Florin is responsible for the app. Privacy contact: cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

You can play without an account. Game progress stays on your device. Advertising and Google's consent requests are enabled only for players who choose “18 or older.” Players choosing “Under 18” or “Prefer not to say” receive no ad requests.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores completed days, scores, stars, game coins, upgrades, settings and an unfinished restaurant day so you can continue playing. These records do not require a name, email address, account or internet connection.

The app also stores your broad privacy age-group choice, whether it has opened before, and Google's consent records. It does not ask for a date of birth. Game saves and these settings are not automatically uploaded to a developer server. The app provides no cloud sync and opts out of Android cloud backup and device transfer.

2. Adult advertising and Google's services

For eligible adults, the app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for an optional startup ad and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy messages and choices. Ad requests follow UMP's eligibility decision. The first launch is ad-free, and ads do not award game coins.

When used, Google may process an IP address and approximate location inferred from it; app/ad interactions; performance and diagnostic data; and device, app-set or account identifiers available to its SDK. These support ad delivery, measurement, SDK operation and fraud prevention. App-set identifiers are for permitted non-advertising uses such as fraud prevention.

This app removes Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions. Identifier availability also depends on Android, device settings and Google's SDK configuration. Google encrypts SDK data in transit using TLS. Google and its advertising service providers handle data under their own privacy notices, including processing outside your country. The developer may receive aggregate advertising reports.

3. Children and players whose age is unspecified

The game has a mixed-age audience. Its neutral privacy screen offers “Under 18,” “18 or older,” and “Prefer not to say,” with no preselected answer or game reward for a particular choice. Under-18 and unspecified players can play without ads. The app does not start UMP consent requests, initialize the advertising SDK for ad loading, or request ads for those players. A missing age choice also prevents ad requests.

Children's progress remains on the device. The app does not ask children for contact details. A parent or guardian can contact the developer about privacy concerns; please do not send a child's full name, birth date or other unnecessary details.

4. Your privacy choices and permissions

Open Settings → Privacy age group to change the local age choice. Adults can use Settings → Privacy choices when Google requires that entry point. It opens Google's form to manage the available consent options. Changing your age choice does not trigger an ad during the current session.

You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions. Opening this policy or another external link uses your browser and that site's privacy practices.

5. Sharing, security and support

The app has no developer-operated gameplay backend, social sharing or account service. The developer does not sell game-save data. Adult advertising involves the Google services above. Local files use Android's app-private storage. Google's SDK network connections are encrypted; no storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

If you email the developer, your address and information you choose to send are used to answer the enquiry, resolve support issues, process privacy requests and meet legal obligations. Email service providers process that correspondence. Do not send passwords or sensitive information that is not needed for the request.

6. Retention and deletion

Local saves and settings remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. To erase them, use Android Settings → Apps → Pizza Glow (or Pizzeria) → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Device labels may differ. This also resets progress, game coins, upgrades and local privacy choices.

There is no game account to delete and no developer server copy of your game save. Clearing local storage does not delete information previously processed by Google. Google's retention and deletion rules are described in its privacy policy and privacy and deletion tools.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry, resolve related issues or meet legal obligations. You may request its deletion by email. Google and the hosting provider apply their own retention rules to data they process.

7. This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends technical request information, including an IP address, to GitHub to retrieve it. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page contains no developer-added analytics, ads, tracking scripts, forms, cookies or third-party fonts. It can be read without a GitHub account.

8. Privacy requests and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent. Use the in-app privacy choices for Google's available controls and email the developer for information handled directly by the developer. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.
